What we look for

The damage behind every inspection plan

Corrosion and wall loss

Thickness mapping on pipes, vessels and tank shells before minimum wall is reached.

Cracking

Surface and sub-surface cracks at welds, nozzles and high-stress areas.

Weld flaws

Porosity, slag and lack of fusion caught at fabrication, repair and turnaround.

Erosion and thinning

Flow-related loss in elbows, headers and process lines, tracked over time.

Questions

Before you enrol

Do I need prior NDT experience?

Level I courses need no prior NDT experience. Level II builds on Level I training and logged experience hours. Phased Array and TOFD expect UT Level II.

Is the course a certification?

You receive a training certificate. For CSWIP 3.1, the examination and certificate come from the CSWIP certification body. NDT personnel certification under SNT-TC-1A or ISO 9712 also needs documented experience and an examination, issued through your employer's written practice or an independent certification body. We explain the route that suits you.

Can you train our team on site?

Yes. Corporate batches can run at your plant or yard, with a syllabus adjusted to the methods and codes you use.

What should I bring?

An ID, your previous training or experience records if you have them, and safety footwear for practical sessions. Course material is provided.

Are there vision requirements?

Yes, a basic near-vision and colour-vision check is standard for NDT personnel. We can arrange it as part of enrolment.

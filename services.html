UT

Ultrasonic testing

Sound waves measure wall thickness and reveal internal flaws and corrosion mapping, without taking equipment out of service.

Finds
Internal flaws, laminations, wall thinning
Best for
Pipes, vessels, tanks, plate
Codes
ASME V, API 510 / 570 / 653, ISO 17640
Enquire about UT
RT

Radiographic testing

Gamma, X-ray and digital radiography show volumetric weld defects such as porosity, slag and lack of fusion. Barriers and controlled exposure keep the site safe.

Finds
Porosity, slag, lack of fusion, cracks
Best for
Pipe welds, castings, thick sections
Codes
ASME V Art. 2, ISO 17636
Enquire about RT
MPI

Magnetic particle inspection

Magnetise the part, apply particles, and surface cracks light up. Wet fluorescent and dry methods for ferromagnetic steel.

Finds
Surface and near-surface cracks
Best for
Ferromagnetic welds, nozzles, forgings
Codes
ASTM E1444, ISO 17638
Enquire about MPI
LPT

Liquid penetrant testing

Visible and fluorescent dye penetrant for surface-breaking defects on any non-porous material, including stainless steel and aluminium.

Finds
Surface-breaking cracks and porosity
Best for
Stainless steel, aluminium, non-magnetic alloys
Codes
ASTM E165, ISO 3452
Enquire about LPT
PAUT

Phased array and TOFD

Advanced ultrasonics with recorded, repeatable scans. Faster than film, with no radiation exclusion zone, and ideal for critical welds.

Finds
Volumetric flaws, flaw height and depth
Best for
Critical welds, thick-wall components
Codes
ISO 13588, ISO 10863, ASME V Art. 4
Enquire about PAUT
Rope access

Rope access inspection

Inspection by rope-trained technicians on tank shells, stacks and flare booms, often with no scaffold build time or cost.

Finds
The same defects as the method used, at height
Best for
Tank shells, stacks, flare booms, jetties
Codes
IRATA-trained teams
Enquire about rope access

Rail welds, wheels and axles, bogies, bridges, workshops

RDSO and railway client specifications
